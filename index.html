Flow is speed. Rolling downhill speeds you up, uphill slows you down. In the air, lean to spin the bike. Land with your wheels lined up with the slope — on the downside of a jump — and you keep your speed. Land flat and you lose some. Land way off and you crash.

Pick your speed. Too slow and you come up short of a landing; too fast and you fly past it onto the flat. Ease off the gas or tap the brake before a jump to land it just right.

Lean back as you leave a jump to pop higher; lean forward to stay low. Lean forward over rollers to soak them up. Land a full flip for a speed boost. Mud slows you down. Online riders are ghosts — you ride right through each other.

On phones: hold your phone sideways. Left thumb leans, right thumb has GAS and BRAKE.
