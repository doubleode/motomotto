HAT TRICK MOTO

Stack hats. Hit jumps. Don't overheat.

How to Play

ZThrottle
XTurbo — faster, but heats the engine
Up / DownChange lanes (on the ground)
LeftLean back (wheelie / nose up in the air)
RightLean forward (nose down in the air)
EscPause / menu

Land with your bike matching the slope of the ground for a clean landing and a speed boost. Land badly and you tumble. If the TEMP gauge fills up, your engine overheats and stalls for 3 seconds — ride over the blue cooling strips to reset it. Mud slows you down, hay bales and barriers knock you off, and bumping into another rider's back wheel sends you flying.

On phones: hold your phone sideways. Left side = lanes and leaning, right side = GAS and TURBO.
